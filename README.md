# Nora Vieytes

K-12 ESOL educator and applied researcher. I build, deploy and evaluate AI for multilingual learners.

### What I work on

- **Early-warning analytics.** The Longitudinal Growth Stagnation Framework (LGSF) flags English learners whose language growth has stalled, before the school year begins. U.S. patent pending (App. No. 19/762,965). Prospective validation pre-registered on OSF: https://osf.io/ry9u5
- **Generative AI for multilingual learners.** Classroom tools built with large language models and evaluated with the same rigor. First project: [Next-Step Writing Feedback](https://github.com/noravieytes/writing-feedback-assistant), asset-based writing feedback organized by the WIDA dimensions of language ([live demo](https://noravieytes.github.io/writing-feedback-assistant/)).
- **Responsible AI in school districts.** Member of the Winchester Public Schools AI Task Force (Virginia). Human-in-the-loop design, FERPA-aligned data practices, NIST AI Risk Management Framework.

### Background
